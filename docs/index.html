What you get

  • Your topics: keywords per topic, each topic separately, plus papers that combine topics.
  • Researchers you follow: their new papers and preprints first, by name or ORCID.
  • Preprints: bioRxiv and medRxiv, filtered by your keywords and categories.
  • Bluesky: new posts from researchers you follow, and optionally posts linking to papers on your topics.
  • Everything free shown: title, authors, journal, date, study type, matched keywords, DOI, PubMed and free full-text links, and the key results from the abstract.
  • Optional AI summaries: aim, methods, results, discussion and caveats for each paper, with your own API key.

Questions

How do I get weekly PubMed alerts by email?

Pick a setup above, list your keywords, and turn email notifications on. You get one email a week with new PubMed papers per topic, plus bioRxiv and medRxiv preprints.

Can I follow specific researchers?

Yes. Add names as "Lastname Initials" or an ORCID. Their new papers, preprints and Bluesky posts come first.

Can I add papers to Zotero?

Copy a DOI from the digest and paste it into Zotero's Add Item by Identifier (the magic-wand button). Several at once works too.

Why not Google Scholar or X?

Google Scholar has no API that tools like this can use, and X charges for every post read through its API. Bluesky's API is open and free, so it is included.

Is it free?

Yes. Only the optional AI summaries use your own API key.
